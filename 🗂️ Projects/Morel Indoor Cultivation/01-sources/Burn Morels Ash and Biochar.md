# Burn morels, wood ash, biochar
- Fire-associated species: *M. sextelata*, *M. tomentosa*, *M. eximia*, *M. exuberans* (PMC6106076). *M. importuna* and *M. exuberans* are facultative.
- Wood ash raised *M. sextelata* yield and quality in a field study (PMC11676116). Potassium proposed as a factor.
- Biochar 0 / 7.5 / 15 / 30 t/ha in sandy loam raised *M. sextelata* yield, linked to soil nitrate and fewer fungal pathogens (PMID 40913927, Microbiological Research 2026).
- Doses are field-scale. Indoor effect untested.
- Reasons for burn-site fruiting are not settled (mineral release, community shift, nitrogen pulse).
