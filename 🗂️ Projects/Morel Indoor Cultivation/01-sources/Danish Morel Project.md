# Danish Morel Project
https://thedanishmorelproject.com/the-morel-project/
- Black morels fruited indoors since 2005. 73 of 80 isolated wild variants fruited (about 90%).
- Best variants about 4.2 kg/m2 in 22 weeks (variant 195) and 4.1 kg/m2 in 20 weeks (variant 234).
- Controlled climate chambers, pallet system, proprietary soil, nutrient substrate and climate programme. Strains and full protocol not public.
