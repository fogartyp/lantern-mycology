# Morel Planting & Harvest Calendar by State (2026)
PDF supplied by Patrick. Outdoor-bed calendar by state. Stage table (see [[00-protocols/Stage Targets]]): spawn storage 36-39F; sow at or below 65F; colonize 55-68F; vernalize 41-55F; dormancy under 40F; trigger 45-59F at ~85% RH; fruit 65-82F at 75-85% RH.
- Outdoor use. Cell numbers are long-term average monthly low-high outdoor **air** temperatures in F for a whole state or region, not a forecast. Rule in the intro: sow when average highs fall through 65F, expect fruiting when they climb back through it.
- Legend (read from Patrick's screenshot, 2026-11-08). Cell color = active stage that month: light blue Sow (<=65F, ~1 mo), teal Colonize (55-68F, 1-2 mo), lavender Vernalize (41-55F), slate Dormancy (<40F, no active growth, does not count toward grow time), pale green Trigger (45-59F, ~85% RH, 2-4 wk, deep soak once soil 4 in down reaches target), green Fruit (65-82F, 75-85% RH, up to 2 mo, water in the morning only), gray off-cycle. Sun icon = warmer than ideal (above 68F), shade cloth helps. Snowflake = too cold for the stage, row cover or greenhouse helps.
- Colonize note: look for white surface patches ~6-8 in across. Note the guide mixes air temperature (calendar) with soil temperature at 4 in (trigger).
