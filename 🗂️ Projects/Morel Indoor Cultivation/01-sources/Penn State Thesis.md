# Penn State thesis - indoor *Morchella* cultivation
https://etda.libraries.psu.edu/catalog/16834szg43
- In vitro: best mycelial growth and pseudosclerotia at **20-25C, dark, pH 5-6** (*M. rufobrunnea*, *M. importuna*). *M. americana* and *M. exuberans* did not form pseudosclerotia in tests.
- Indoor trials used Chinese outdoor techniques. Mature *M. rufobrunnea* fruited from soil+straw and soil+sawdust in experiment 1, not in experiment 2 (many primordia, none matured).
- Related SARE project (LNE19-389R, https://projects.sare.org/project-reports/lne19-389r/): indoor trials used only *M. importuna* because *M. rufobrunnea* spawn bags were contaminated. It advised against investing heavily in morels.
