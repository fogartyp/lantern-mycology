# Ceramyca PODs
https://ceramyca.com/pods
- Reusable terracotta cylinder holding mycelium, pushed into outdoor soil; lid opening directs growth to the surface. Gardener POD (self-contained) and POD Pro (connects a grain bag).
- Inoculate in autumn at 18C falling (importuna, sextelata, eximia); rufobrunnea at midsummer.
