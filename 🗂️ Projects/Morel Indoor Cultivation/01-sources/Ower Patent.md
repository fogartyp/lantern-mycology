# Ower patent family (US 4,594,809 and follow-ons)
- US 4,594,809 "Cultivation of Morchella", filed 1985-04-29, issued 1986-06-17. Inventors Ronald D. Ower, Gary L. Mills, James A. Malachowski. Assignee Neogen.
- Follow-ons: US 4,757,640 (1988), US 4,866,878 (1989).
- Method: grow mycelium and sclerotia on a nutrient-rich medium, move to a nutrient-poor substrate, trigger fruiting with heavy water. Indoor, year-round.
- Mushroom Growers' Newsletter reports others could not reproduce it from the text; Terry Farms (Alabama) ran it commercially until 1999.
- Status: 4,594,809 listed as expired. By standard patent terms all three should have lapsed. **Legal status of the other two not verified.**
