# US 11,925,146 - morel cultivation without nutrient bag (China)
https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/11925146
- Soil humidity 20-30%, soil temp 4-18C during cultivation.
- Indoor fruiting ventilation: 1-2x/day for 25-35 min at 0.05-0.2 m/s early; 2-4x/day for 50-70 min at 0.05-0.3 m/s as fruits grow.
- Air humidity 85-95%, soil humidity 25-30%, surface temp 8-20C at the 5-13 cm stage.
- **Current patent.** Check before any commercial use.
