# Masaphy 2010 - soilless system, *M. rufobrunnea*
Biotechnology Letters 32(10):1523-7. PMID 20563623. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20563623
- Sclerotia phase: mycelium on sterilized (1 h at 120C) limestone-buffered potting soil over a nutrient-rich wheat-grain layer, 2-3 weeks at 18-25C.
- Chill sclerotia layer at 4C until use.
- Trigger: continuous watering 5-24 h.
- Fruit: 16-22C, ~90% RH. Initials in 2-4 weeks; mature 7-15 cm.
- Sclerotia germinated to mycelium within 3-6 days of watering, then a conidial layer, then knots, then fruit bodies.
