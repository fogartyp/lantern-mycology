## Guides and vendor material (vendor claims are marketing until tested)
| Resource | Notes | Read status |
|---|---|---|
| [North Spore: Ultimate Morel Growing Guide](https://northspore.com/blogs/the-black-trumpet/morel-growing-guide) | Jonathan Carver, published 2026-02-13. Overview of biology, history and two cultivation paths (landscape patch vs managed bed). Says morel cultivation is still developing and much of it is "current thinking and common practice rather than settled fact". Vendor guide. | Opening sections only; rest of page truncated |
| [Mushroom Appreciation: Morel species](https://www.mushroom-appreciation.com/morel-species.html) | Species identification for North America (18 described species per the page). Useful for ID and for knowing which species our strains are. Says *M. sextelata* and *M. septimelata* are the "greenie" burn morels, and *M. rufobrunnea* is the saprobic white morel that "can be cultivated" but is "not commercially viable yet". Foraging oriented, not a cultivation source. | Most of the page; middle section omitted |
