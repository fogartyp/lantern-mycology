# Stage targets

Conflicting values are kept side by side on purpose. Test both, log which works.
Source notes: [[Morel Planting Guide 2026]], [[Penn State Thesis]], [[US11925146 Chinese Patent]], [[Masaphy 2010]], [[Ower Patent]], [[Danish Morel Project]].

| Stage | Target | Duration | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spawn storage | 36-39F (fridge) | until planting | [[Morel Planting Guide 2026]] | vendor guide |
| Sclerotia / mycelium growth (lab) | 20-25C (68-77F), dark, pH 5-6 | weeks | [[Penn State Thesis]] | thesis (lab, agar) |
| Sclerotia on grain/sawdust (vendor) | 60-65F, dark | a few weeks | vendor product page | vendor |
| Sow | air at or below 65F | ~1 month | [[Morel Planting Guide 2026]] | vendor guide |
| Colonize | 55-68F | 1-2 months | [[Morel Planting Guide 2026]] | vendor guide |
| Colonize (Chinese method) | soil 4-18C, soil moisture 20-30% | 40-60 days | [[US11925146 Chinese Patent]] | patent |
| Vernalize (cold) | 41-55F | varies | [[Morel Planting Guide 2026]] | vendor guide |
| Dormancy (outdoor) | below 40F, no active growth; does not count toward grow time | varies | [[Morel Planting Guide 2026]] | vendor guide (outdoor air) |
| Trigger | 45-59F, ~85% RH, deep soak | 2-4 weeks | [[Morel Planting Guide 2026]] | vendor guide |
| Trigger (soilless) | continuous watering 5-24 h after 4C chill | - | [[Masaphy 2010]] | peer-reviewed |
| Fruit | 65-82F air, 75-85% RH | up to 2 months | [[Morel Planting Guide 2026]] | vendor guide (outdoor air temps) |
| Fruit (Chinese patent) | surface 4-20C, air RH 85-95%, soil moisture 25-30% | - | [[US11925146 Chinese Patent]] | patent |
| Fruit (soilless) | 16-22C, ~90% RH | 2-4 wk to initials | [[Masaphy 2010]] | peer-reviewed |
| Air exchange (indoor) | 0.05-0.3 m/s, 1-4 times/day, 25-70 min | - | [[US11925146 Chinese Patent]] | patent |

## Notes
- Outdoor guides use **outdoor air temperature**; indoor protocols use **soil/surface temperature**. Log both.
- Soil pH target in vendor guides is about 7.0-7.5; lab sclerotia work prefers pH 5-6 for mycelium. These may apply to different stages. Unresolved.
- Wood ash raises pH. Re-test after amending.
